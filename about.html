ABOUT IWS

INDIAN WELDING SOCIETY - KEC STUDENT FORUM

THE IWS STUDENT FORUM PROVIDES A PLATFORM FOR STUDENTS INTERESTED IN WELDING AND RELATED TECHNOLOGIES TO LEARN, EXPLORE AND DEVELOP THEIR TECHNICAL INTERESTS.

THROUGH TECHNICAL ACTIVITIES, LEARNING OPPORTUNITIES AND EVENTS, THE SOCIETY ENCOURAGES STUDENTS TO ENGAGE WITH WELDING TECHNOLOGY AND ITS PRACTICAL APPLICATIONS.
