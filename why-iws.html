WHY IWS?

WHERE CURIOSITY BECOMES SKILL, IDEAS BECOME INNOVATION, AND STUDENTS BECOME FUTURE ENGINEERS.

MORE THAN A SOCIETY

EVERY GREAT ENGINEER STARTS WITH A SPARK.

SOMETIMES THAT SPARK IS A QUESTION. SOMETIMES IT IS A FAILURE. SOMETIMES IT IS A SIMPLE CURIOSITY ABOUT HOW SOMETHING WORKS.

IWS IS A PLATFORM WHERE THAT CURIOSITY CAN GROW INTO KNOWLEDGE, EXPERIENCE, CONFIDENCE AND INNOVATION.

WE BRING STUDENTS TOGETHER TO LEARN, EXPLORE WELDING TECHNOLOGY AND EXPERIENCE THE REAL WORLD OF ENGINEERING.

LEARN. CREATE. WELD. LEAD.

WELDING CONNECTS MATERIALS. IWS CONNECTS PEOPLE, IDEAS AND OPPORTUNITIES.
